Transaction and investment support

Buying, selling or raising capital against a mining asset — with the financial due diligence done by someone who knows how mining numbers are built, and where they bend.

Book a 30-minute call The 21-Day Review

The problem

Mining assets are unusually easy to misprice. Reported EBITDA depends on stripping capitalisation choices, stockpile valuation, rehabilitation provisioning and hedge accounting — every one of which is a judgement, and several of which are reversible by a buyer with a different view.

On top of that sits a fiscal position that is no longer stable. A Ghanaian gold asset valued on a flat 5% royalty is valued on a regime that stopped existing in March 2026. A South African asset's effective rate depends on the refined or unrefined classification of its output and on whether capital expenditure is correctly ring-fenced to the right mine under section 36(7F).

Generalist diligence teams test the arithmetic. They are much less likely to test whether the mining judgements underneath it are defensible.

What we do

Financial due diligence focused on the things that move the price. We rebuild quality of earnings from the ledger rather than accepting the vendor's adjusted figure, and we test the specifically mining judgements: stripping, stockpiles, rehabilitation, royalty basis, capex ring-fencing, intercompany pricing on marketing and procurement entities.

On sell-side, the same work done early and honestly, so that the findings surface in your data room rather than in the buyer's diligence report two weeks before signing.

What you receive

  • Financial due diligence report — findings ranked by effect on value
  • Quality of earnings analysis — normalised, with every adjustment evidenced
  • Fiscal position review — royalty, tax and levy exposure under the regime actually in force, not the one in the information memorandum
  • Model review — mechanics, assumptions and sensitivities
  • 100-day finance integration plan — for buy-side, what the finance function has to do from completion

How it is billed

Per transaction, fixed fee against a defined scope and timetable. Deal timetables slip; we price the scope, not the calendar, and tell you up front what a compressed timetable does to the depth of work.

Who this is for

Acquirers of producing or development-stage African mining assets, vendors preparing for a sale, and companies raising debt or equity where the lender or investor will run diligence on your numbers anyway.

Questions

Do you do valuation?

We do the financial diligence that a valuation rests on. Formal valuation opinions and competent persons' reports on reserves and resources are separate disciplines with their own qualification requirements, and we will refer you rather than overreach.

Can you work to a four-week timetable?

Often, with a tighter scope. We would rather agree a narrower scope done properly than a full scope done badly, and we will say which on the first call.

Will you work alongside our legal advisers?

Yes, routinely. Financial diligence and legal diligence answer different questions and need to be read together.
