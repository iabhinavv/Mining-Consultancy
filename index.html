Why this matters now

2026 rewrote the rules. Most finance teams are closing the year on 2025 assumptions.

Four fiscal changes landed in Ghana this year and the Minerals Commission ran its first mining audit in a decade. If your budget, royalty returns and group pack were built before March, your first full year under the new regime closes in about two months.

Royalty

The flat 5% is gone

Gold royalty now moves with the price, from 5% up to 12% above $4,500 an ounce. Every return, accrual and forecast built on a fixed rate is wrong.

Reporting

No single set of numbers

Five to ten subsidiaries, several currencies and charts of accounts, and a consolidation that takes weeks. The audit asked for three years of financial records.

Liquidity

Cash tied to a commodity price

Capex is fixed, revenue is not — and now your royalty rate moves with the price too. A drop hits you twice.

Cost

Procurement spend nobody owns

Fuel, reagents, spares and contractors are bought site by site, with little leverage and less visibility.

Questions

What finance directors ask first.

Are you a licensed audit firm?

No. Amrev is a finance and tax advisory practice, not a licensed audit firm, and we do not sign statutory audit opinions. We prepare your group for audit, build the consolidation and fix the gaps an auditor would find. Your registered statutory auditor signs the opinion, and we work alongside them.

Is your tax work legal advice?

No. Our work is financial and tax advisory. Where a matter needs legal sign-off we work with local counsel in the relevant country.

What do you need from us to start?

Less than most people expect. We work from documents your finance team already produces: trial balance, royalty returns, bank statements, the group reporting pack and your last audit file. No system access, and no new reporting built for our benefit.

How does the success fee on procurement savings work?

We agree a spending baseline with you before we start. The success fee is a percentage of savings measured against that baseline and verified together, so you only pay it on savings that actually show up.

What currency do you invoice in?

US dollars. Projects are billed in milestones, with part of the fee at the start and the balance on delivery.

Can you work with an exploration company that is not yet producing?

Yes. The junior miner arrangement combines a reduced cash fee with a small equity participation, agreed case by case.

Are the figures in the dashboard real client numbers?

No. The dashboard at the top of this page uses illustrative figures to show the kind of reporting we build. Client data is never published.

What happens on the first call?

You tell us what is not working. We tell you whether we can help, what a first engagement would look like, and roughly what it would involve. There is no charge and no obligation.
