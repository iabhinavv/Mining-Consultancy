What we usually find

Four problems show up in almost every mid-tier miner.

Too big for a local bookkeeping firm, too small to pay Big Four fees every quarter. The finance team is stretched, and the gaps sit between countries and entities.

Compliance

Rules move faster than filings

Royalty rates, withholding rules and export requirements change across jurisdictions, often with little notice.

Reporting

No single set of numbers

Five to ten subsidiaries, several currencies and charts of accounts, and a consolidation that takes weeks.

Liquidity

Cash tied to a commodity price

Capex is fixed, revenue is not. A price drop turns a comfortable quarter into a covenant conversation.

Cost

Procurement spend nobody owns

Fuel, reagents, spares and contractors are bought site by site, with little leverage and less visibility.

Questions

What finance directors ask first.

Do you sign statutory audit opinions?

No. We prepare your group for audit, build the consolidation and fix the gaps an auditor would find. Your registered statutory auditor signs the opinion, and we work alongside them.

Is your tax work legal advice?

No. Our work is financial and tax advisory. Where a matter needs legal sign-off, we work with local counsel in the relevant country.

How does the success fee on procurement savings work?

We agree a spending baseline with you before we start. The success fee is a percentage of savings measured against that baseline and verified together, so you only pay it on savings that actually show up.

What currency do you invoice in?

US dollars. Projects are billed in milestones, with part of the fee at the start and the balance on delivery.

Can you work with an exploration company that is not yet producing?

Yes. The junior miner arrangement combines a reduced cash fee with a small equity participation, agreed case by case.

Are the figures in the dashboard real client numbers?

No. The dashboard at the top of this page uses illustrative figures to show the kind of reporting we build. Client data is never published.

What happens on the first call?

You tell us what is not working. We tell you whether we can help, what a first engagement would look like, and roughly what it would involve. There is no charge and no obligation.
