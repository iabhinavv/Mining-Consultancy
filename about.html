About

A mining finance practice, based in Accra

Amrev exists because mid-tier African mining groups sit in a gap: too complex for a local bookkeeping firm, too small to spend Big Four money every quarter, and reporting across jurisdictions nobody treats as a single problem.

Why multi-country consolidation is the thread

Consolidating a finance function across Europe, China, Mexico and the USA is, structurally, the same problem as consolidating a mining group across Ghana, South Africa and two holding jurisdictions: different charts of accounts, different currencies, different local rules, and a group number that has to be defensible to an auditor who will test it.

African mining groups face that problem with considerably less support than a global bank gets. That is the gap Amrev was set up to close.

What we are, precisely

A finance and tax advisory practice. We recalculate royalty and tax positions, build consolidations and cash models, design internal audit frameworks, analyse procurement spend, and run financial due diligence on transactions.

What we are not

We would rather state this plainly here than have it discovered later.

  • Not a licensed audit firm. Amrev is not licensed by the Institute of Chartered Accountants, Ghana, is not a registered South African audit firm, and does not sign statutory audit opinions. We prepare groups for audit and work alongside the licensed firm that signs.
  • Not a law firm. Our work is financial and tax advisory. Anything requiring legal sign-off goes to local counsel in the relevant country.
  • Not a technical mining consultancy. Reserves, resources, geotechnical and tailings work needs a competent person with the right qualification. We will refer rather than overreach.
  • Not a large team. If an engagement needs more hands than the practice has, we say so and either sub-contract specialists transparently or decline.

How we price

Fees are quoted after the first call, once we understand the number of entities, jurisdictions and the actual problem. Scope and fee are agreed in writing before any work starts, projects are billed in milestones, and everything is invoiced in USD. Findings are delivered on the agreed date or you don't pay the final instalment.

Where we work

Ghana and South Africa are the two markets we know properly — where we can speak to the detail of the fiscal regime rather than the general shape of it. Other African jurisdictions by arrangement, where the work is mainly consolidation, cash or procurement rather than local fiscal detail.
