Privacy and cookie policy

This explains what personal data we collect through this website and our advisory work, what the advertising tags on this site do, and the rights you have over your data. Last updated 27 October 2026.

1. Who we are

Amrev Consulting Group ("Amrev", "we", "us") is a finance and tax advisory practice at 201, Blohum Rd, Accra, Ghana. We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

Contact us about anything in this policy at experimentsav@gmail.com or +233 25 731 9254.

Registered with the Data Protection Commission of Ghana under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), registration number [DPC REGISTRATION NUMBER].

2. What we collect

If you only read the site

Our host records standard server information including your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. If you consent to analytics or advertising cookies, we and the third parties named below also receive information about your visit — see sections 3 and 4.

This site has no contact form. We do not ask you to type anything into it.

If you contact us or book a call

When you book through Calendly, message us on WhatsApp, call or email, we receive what you choose to give us: your name, email address, phone number, employer, and whatever you tell us about your situation. Calendly also records the time you booked and your time zone.

If you become a client

We receive the documents needed to do the work — trial balances, royalty returns, bank statements, reporting packs. These are mostly company records rather than personal data, but they contain the names and contact details of your staff, and sometimes payroll information. We handle them under the confidentiality terms in your engagement letter.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send us, special categories of personal data such as health information.

3. Cookies and tracking

We use three categories. Only the first is active before you choose.

Category What it does Set by Needs consent
Necessary Remembers your cookie choice so we don't ask again. Stored in your browser, not sent to us. Amrev No
Analytics Tells us which pages are read and how people arrive, so we can improve the site. Google Yes
Advertising Measures whether our ads work and allows ads to be targeted to you on other sites and apps. Google, Meta Yes

Analytics and advertising tags are not loaded at all until you agree to them. If you reject them, or ignore the banner, they never run. You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page, and withdrawing is as easy as giving consent.

Fonts on this site are served by Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads. This happens before any cookie choice because it is part of rendering the page. If you would rather that did not happen, most browsers allow you to block requests to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com.

4. Advertising, the Meta pixel and Google tags

If you consent to advertising cookies, this site uses pixels, web beacons and similar storage technologies from third parties, including Meta, to collect or receive information from this website and elsewhere on the internet. Those third parties, including Meta, use that information to provide measurement services and to target and deliver advertising to you.

How to opt out. You can refuse or withdraw consent at any time through the Cookie settings link in our footer, which stops these tags running on this site. To opt out of interest-based advertising more broadly, including on other websites, you can use:

  • aboutads.info/choices — the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out
  • youronlinechoices.eu — the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out
  • Your Meta account settings, under Ad preferences
  • Google Ad Settings

Where you are in a country whose law requires consent before cookies are stored, we pass your choices to Google using Google Consent Mode v2, and we do not initialise the Meta pixel until you have consented.

We do not send Meta or Google any information about your finances, your company's financial position, or any document you send us. The advertising tags see page visits, not the substance of our work.

5. Our lawful basis

  • Consent — analytics and advertising cookies, and marketing emails.
  • Performance of a contract — the personal data inside the documents we process for a client engagement.
  • Legitimate interests — responding to an enquiry you send us, keeping our site secure, and keeping records of the advice we have given. We think these are interests you would expect and that do not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation — records we have to keep under Ghanaian tax and company law.

7. International transfers

The providers above are mostly outside Ghana and South Africa, principally in the United States and the European Union. Where we transfer personal data outside Ghana we rely on your consent or on the contract with you, and we use providers that offer recognised safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Section 18 of Act 843 and Chapter 9 of POPIA govern these transfers.

8. How long we keep it

  • Cookie choice — 180 days, then we ask again
  • Enquiries that do not become engagements — 12 months
  • Client records — six years after the engagement ends, to meet professional and tax record-keeping requirements
  • Advertising event data held by Meta — Meta retains this for up to two years under its Business Tools Terms

9. Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and, where it applies, South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and the UK/EU GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct it if it is wrong, or complete it if it is incomplete
  • delete it, where we have no continuing reason to hold it
  • stop processing it for direct marketing, which we will always do
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • withdraw consent you have given, at any time

Email experimentsav@gmail.com. We will respond within the period the applicable law requires, and we will not charge you for a first request.

10. Marketing

We only send marketing email to people who have asked for it, or who are existing clients and were given the chance to decline when we took their details. Every message has an unsubscribe link.

If you are in South Africa, section 69 of POPIA prohibits direct marketing by electronic communication without your prior consent unless you are an existing customer, and the amended POPIA Regulations in force since 17 April 2025 confirm that an opt-out does not count as consent. We treat that as the standard everywhere, not only for South African contacts.

11. Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Client documents are held in access-controlled storage and shared only with people working on your engagement. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

12. Complaints

Please raise it with us first — it is usually quicker. If you are not satisfied you can complain to:

  • Ghana — the Data Protection Commission, dataprotection.org.gh
  • South Africa — the Information Regulator, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, P.O. Box 31533, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2017
  • UK or EU — your national data protection authority

13. Changes

If we change how we use personal data we will update this page and, where the change is material, ask for your cookie choices again. The version date at the top tells you when it last changed.
