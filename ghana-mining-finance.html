What changed in Ghana in 2026

Four fiscal changes took effect this year, and the Minerals Commission ran a physical and financial audit across the sector. Taken together they are the largest shift in Ghanaian mining finance in a decade.

Change Detail In force
Sliding-scale mineral royalty L.I. 2517 abolished the flat 5%. Gold pays a minimum 5% below $2,000/oz, rising to a maximum 12% above $4,500/oz. Lithium moved to its own 5–12% scale by spodumene price. 10 Mar 2026
Growth and Sustainability Levy cut GSL on the gold mining sector reduced from 3% to 1%, to offset the higher royalty burden. 31 Mar 2026
Mandatory e-invoicing All VAT-registered businesses must issue invoices through GRA-certified software, transmitted in real time. The e-invoice is the only valid document for claiming input tax credit. Non-onboarding risks automatic suspension of VAT registration. 1 Jul 2026
GRA Integrated Tax Administration System ITAS replaces the Taxpayer's Portal and GITMIS, with a national rollout. Sep 2026
Minerals Commission sector audit Physical and financial audit. Miners required to submit ten years of production logs, three years of financial records and all permits. Nov 2025 – Jun 2026

The full detail, with every source, is in the 2026 fiscal change register.

What it means for your finance function

The royalty line is no longer a constant

A flat 5% could sit in a model as a hard-coded percentage. A sliding scale cannot. Your accrual, your budget, your covenant headroom and your 13-week cash forecast all now need the gold price assumption to drive the royalty rate. Most models built before March 2026 do not do this, and the error compounds in the direction of understating cost when the price rises.

Two changes move in opposite directions

The royalty went up for most producers; the GSL came down from 3% to 1%. The net effect on any particular group depends on its realised price and its profitability, which means the only honest answer to "are we better or worse off?" comes from recalculating it rather than estimating it.

Ten years of production logs is a reporting test

The audit asked for records at a depth most finance functions have never had to produce at short notice. Groups with a documented consolidation and an organised audit file produced them. Groups without one learned how much of their reporting depended on one person and one spreadsheet.

E-invoicing is now a cash risk, not an IT project

Because the e-invoice is the only valid document for claiming input tax credit, an onboarding gap does not just create a compliance problem — it puts VAT recovery at risk, which shows up directly in working capital.
